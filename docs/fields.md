## Rules beyond presence

- minimum: no citation and no DOI
- minimum: public resource with no access point URL
- minimum: restricted/internal resource without access_note (how to obtain it)
- minimum: licence unknown: give an SPDX id or at least the text of the terms
- minimum: every access point needs a kind (raster, vector, tabular, document, mixed, other) and a method (download, landing_page, service, request)
- recommended: citation without structured authors and no DOI
- recommended: period without a machine-readable start
- recommended: no bounding box
- recommended: no contact with the licensor role
- recommended: non-standard licence without the URL of its terms
- recommended: no keyword linked to a vocabulary (e.g. AGROVOC)
- profile `mosaic`: id `MFL-YYYY-NNN` and `mosaic` with `landscapes`
